Genie Dock

The three minimise effects as recovered from macOS 27.0 Dock.app, drawn with the same mesh arithmetic. Click the window to minimise it and click the tile to bring it back. Hold Shift while clicking for the Dock's ten-times slow motion.

Click the window · Shift-click for slow motion · scrub with the slider while paused

What the Dock computes

clock      p = 0.5 − 0.5·cos(π · t / duration),   duration = 500 ms Genie, 200 ms Scale, 400 ms Suck
steps(d)   = floor(ln(d / 2 + 1)) · 50

Genie      L = target.left − window.left,  R = target.right − window.right,  T = window.far − target.far
           A = steps((|L| + |R|) / 8),  B = steps(T),  enter = B · acos(1 − 2·(T − height) / T) / π
           A = max(A, enter),  lead = A − enter,  total = p · (B + lead)
           squeeze c = ramp(clamp(total, 0, A) / A),  slide s = ramp(clamp(total − lead, 0, B) / B)
           edge(y)  = window.edge + L·c·σ(y),  σ(y) = ramp((window.far − y) / round(T))
           rows from window.far − T·s down to max(window.far − T·s − height, target.far),
           subdivided until segments are under 8 pt or within 0.6 pt of straight
           reveal   = clamp(1 − (T − T·s) / (height − target.height), 0, 1)

Scale      e = ramp(p),  corners = window.corners + (target.corners − window.corners) · e

Suck       e = ramp(p),  grid of one line per 70 pt (at most 19),  reach = largest corner displacement
           each point moves toward its place in the target by min(distance, reach · e)

The rows are the real mesh the Dock hands the window server, two points per row for a vertical Dock; the picture is the window image stretched over them. The thumbnail reveal is drawn as the tile filling in. Window chrome, shadows, the tile's icon art and the second arrival pass that fades the window are not part of the recovered model.
